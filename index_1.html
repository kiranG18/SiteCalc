How SiteCalc works

Getting Started

1
Pick a calculator
From Home, tap any calculator — Brickwork, Concrete, Steel/BBS, and more. Use the category chips or search bar to find it faster.
2
Enter your site measurements
Type in dimensions in feet or metres. Some calculators show a live blueprint preview as you type.
3
Get an instant result
See the final quantity right away, plus a full step-by-step working so you can verify every number.
4
Share or save it
Tap Share via WhatsApp to send the result to your supplier or client, or tap PDF to save a measurement sheet.
5
Track it on a project
From any result, tap "Add to Project Phase" to attach it to a floor — it'll show up right there in that project's progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does SiteCalc work offline?
Yes. Every calculation runs on your device — no internet connection needed. You'll only need data to open WhatsApp or view a shared link.
How accurate are the calculations?
Formulas follow standard IS-1200, IS-2502 and IS-456 practice. Orange "Audit Check" notes flag any assumption worth double-checking against your site conditions before ordering material.
Can I use a custom brick size or mortar ratio?
Yes — open the Brickwork calculator and expand "Advanced Options" to set a custom brick size, mortar ratio, and wastage percentage.
How do I delete a project or calculation?
Swipe or tap the trash icon on any project card or history entry. You'll always get a confirmation before anything is removed.
Is SiteCalc free?
Yes — every calculator and feature in SiteCalc is free to use, with no locked or paid options.
